Beat Road

A drive through Japan, laid out by music. Every beat becomes a lantern on the road ahead. Steer through them, and around the traffic. Grab yen coins and green repair crosses too. Your car's health bar drains when you hit things.

How the Spotify mode works
  1. Open open.spotify.com in another tab of this browser and start a song.
  2. Click Play with Spotify, pick the Spotify tab, and make sure "Also share tab audio" is on.
  3. The game mutes that tab and plays its audio here about 2 seconds late. That delay is what lets the road show beats before you hear them.
Spotify doesn't let web apps read the raw audio of a track, so the game captures the tab's audio instead. Skip songs in Spotify as usual. The drive continues until you stop sharing.
